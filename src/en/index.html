01 / 03

What changed,
at a glance.

Four rules cover most of the difference. Each card is a real set of syllables.

01ü → v
吕女

There is no ü on the keyboard. v is already there.

02j/q/x → g/k/h
鸡七西

j, q and x are the least stable letters in English. g/k/h change predictably before i/v.

03-i → y
知资

The apical vowel is not i. Give it its own letter.

04ao/ong/ian
高东烟

Spell the actual vowels: au, ung, ien. No more detours for handwriting.

Also: tone → digit; no y/w glides; no iu/ui/un contractions; üan → ven, ün → vn. The full list is on the scheme page.
